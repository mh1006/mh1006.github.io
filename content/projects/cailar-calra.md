## Product Overview

In Taiwan, there is a growing emphasis on art education, yet a significant gap in students’ understanding and exploration of color concepts. **Cailar & Carla** aimed to bridge this gap by creating an interactive learning product to introduce elementary students to the fundamentals of color theory.

Cailar & Carla is an interactive color learning product designed for elementary students (**ages 7–11**) to explore color theory through hands-on play. 
By integrating a **plush tactile** controller and an accompanying physical **picture book** with a **gamified mobile app**, children can capture colors from their real-world environment to solve challenges. This multimodal approach addresses the lack of engaging, depth-oriented color education in current curriculums.

## Final Design
The product is divided into three parts.
![Product](/image/CarlaCaila/product.png)

### Key Features
* **Tangible Interaction**: A plush-covered device that encourages children (**ages 7–11**) to explore their surroundings physically.
* **Gamified Pedagogy**: Transforms abstract color theory into an engaging story-driven game.
* **Full-Stack Implementation**: Managed the end-to-end data pipeline from physical hardware sensors to an interactive mobile interface, ensuring seamless data flow and system stability.

### Picture Book
Used with the color sensor for an enhanced learning experience.

The story follows a quest to find lost colors, gradually introducing concepts such as color theory, color mixing, HSL, and complementary colors and other related topics. This journey culminates in a lively carnival where characters engage in various color-themed games.

#### Hardware Circuit Design
![Circuit Diagram](/image/CarlaCaila/circuitDiagram.png)
* **Environmental Adaptability**: The integrated **LED Light Reinforcement** compensates for insufficient ambient light.
* **Power Efficiency**: The circuit uses a step-down module to convert 9V input into the precise voltage required for the ESP32 and sensors, ensuring long-term stability.
* **Low-Latency Feedback**: The system is optimized to transmit sensed data to the **Flutter App** immediately upon button press, creating a responsive learning loop for children.

## Product testing

### Participants
* Total: 10 students
* Age range: 7 to 11 years old

### Testing Format
* Conducted in one-on-one or one-on-two sessions
* Each session lasted approximately 40 minutes

### Testing Process
* **Pre-Test:** Assessed students' existing knowledge of color concepts.
* **Storybook Teaching:** Introduced color knowledge through engaging storytelling.
* **Game Experience:** Allowed students to interact with the product and explore game features.
* **Post-Test:**
    * Reassessed students' color knowledge
    * Evaluated learning outcomes
    * Gathered feedback on product usability
    
![Circuit Diagram](/image/CarlaCaila/usabilityTest.png)

Before testing, most students had limited color knowledge, and older students did not necessarily possess more understanding of color concepts.

### What Worked Well
The game offers various difficulty levels.
*  Older students can engage with advanced levels and avoid boredom.
* Younger students can enjoy simpler levels, finding fun in the game.

### What Could Be Enhanced
Simplify color knowledge instruction for better understanding.
* Some students are still confused of the content after the instruction.
* Younger students required additional guidance to understand certain concepts.
Improve button design to make the indicator mark more visible and the button easier to press.


**Overall students provided positive feedback on our product.**

## Tech Stack

### **Hardware / Firmware**
* **Controller**: ESP32 (NodeMCU32S).
* **Sensor**: TCS34725 Color Sensor.
* **Environment**: Arduino IDE (C/C++).
* **Protocol**: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

### **Software / Mobile**
* **Framework**: Flutter (Dart).
* **UI/UX Design**: Adobe Illustrator.
