Ocean

• 4 min read Jul 2023 – Aug 2023

Experience Focused Learning Like Never Before!

Product Overviews

In modern society, phone notifications frequently disrupt our concentration and can even affect our emotional well-being. This constant stream of alerts not only diverts our attention but also results in decreased productivity. Conversely, missing important messages often leads to feelings of anxiety. Therefore, it is essential to find an effective way to achieve balance.

Ocean is an interactive device that helps users reduce anxiety caused by notification interruptions, enabling a more focused and mindful experience while they can receive important messages.

User Research

We developed a survey focusing on FOMO and smartphone addiction, then selected the most relevant respondents for in-depth qualitative interviews.

Survey Participation

48 surveys was filtered and the 6 most relevant was selected.

Persona

The following Personas was created based on the interview results.

Journey Map

The user’s emotional journey and pain points while trying to focus.

Point Of View

  • User: College students currently in their studies.
  • Insight: They feel insecure when they miss important messages.
  • Implication: Timely receipt of important messages is essential for their sense of security.

Concept Development

Brainstorming Solutions

We brainstorm ideas to solve the problem and then use quadrant analysis to evaluate them based on feasibility and creativity.

Choosing a Solution

We chose a concept that is both feasible and creative "the mailbox" and developed related features based on this idea.

Design Goal

Make notifications more manageable by categorizing them like mail, with messages received at different speeds.

Important → Urgent mail
Unimportant → Ordinary mail

Sketch

Despite the implementation of a solution mailbox, the issue of unwanted interruptions from unimportant messages persisted. After discussion and revision, in order to address the issue of excessive unnecessary messages, we linked cell phone messages to message in a bottle.

Message in a bottle

Important message → Float on the sea surface.
Unimportant → Sink to the bottom of the sea.

We also added a spinning cork to create a ritualistic element.

  1. The device of Ocean
    Creating a sense of routine can assist individuals in entering a learning state more efficiently. Users must place their phones on the device to activate the app and adjust the reading time by rotating the bottle cap, which also aids in the creation of a pre-reading ritual.

  2. Simulated message transmission device
    We used two buttons to simulate message transmission from different contacts to the app, allowing us to easily showcase different outcomes.

  3. Sketches of the user interface of Ocean’s app.

Final Design

1. Setting up contacts

Receive messages from up to three contacts

2. Place your phone

Launching the Ocean App will automatically restrict other apps.

3. Set up timer

Press the start button to begin the countdown.

4. Receive messages

Messages will display different statuses depending on the contact.

Important

Message will float on the surface of the sea and display their content.

Unimportant

Messages will sink to the bottom of the sea and disappear.

5. Receive study reward

Immediate positive feedback helps fosters a sense of achievement.

6. Create your own ocean

Visit the store to exchange for decorations and create an under water wonderland.

How to use?

User Journey Map

After using Ocean, emotional fluctuations have stabilized compared to before, enhanced mood and reduced feelings of depression.

Final Product Prototype

In the exhibition, we showcased the product prototype in the following parts:

  1. Ocean Device
    Adjust the reading duration in the app by turning the knob.
  2. User’s Phone
    Place the phone on the device to activate the app.
  3. Phone for Sending Messages from Important Contacts
    Press the red button to send important messages to the phone.
  4. Phone for Sending Messages from Unimportant Contacts
    Press the blue button to send unimportant messages to the phone.
