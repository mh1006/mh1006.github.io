## Product Overviews
In modern society, phone notifications frequently disrupt our concentration and can even affect our emotional well-being. This constant stream of alerts not only diverts our attention but also results in decreased productivity. Conversely, missing important messages often leads to feelings of anxiety. Therefore, it is essential to find an effective way to achieve balance.

**Ocean** is an interactive device that helps users reduce anxiety caused by notification interruptions, enabling a more focused and mindful experience while they can receive important messages.

## User Research

## Design Goal
Make notifications more manageable by categorizing them like mail, with messages received at different speeds.

Important → Urgent mail </br>
Unimportant →  Ordinary mail

---

## Sketch
Despite the implementation of a solution mailbox, the issue of unwanted interruptions from unimportant messages persisted. After discussion and revision, in order to address the issue of excessive unnecessary messages, we linked cell phone messages to message in a bottle.
### Message in a bottle
**Important message →  Float on the sea surface.** </br>
**Unimportant →  Sink to the bottom of the sea.**

We also added a spinning cork to create a ritualistic element.

1. **The device of Ocean** </br>
Creating a sense of routine can assist individuals in entering a learning state more efficiently. Users must place their phones on the device to activate the app and adjust the reading time by rotating the bottle cap, which also aids in the creation of a pre-reading ritual.

2. **Simulated message transmission device** </br>
We used two buttons to simulate message transmission from different contacts to the app, allowing us to easily showcase different outcomes.
